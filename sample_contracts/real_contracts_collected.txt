REAL CONTRACTS COLLECTED FROM SEC EDGAR (PUBLIC FILINGS)
==========================================================
These are excerpts of real, publicly-filed executed contracts, pulled from
SEC EDGAR exhibits (material contracts attached to public company filings).
They are public record — not privileged client data. Quotes below are verbatim
from the filed documents; sections not quoted were not located or not present
in the exhibit.


==========================================================
CONTRACT 1: MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
==========================================================
Source: Aspira Women's Health, Inc. — 8-K exhibit EX-10.1
Filed: 2025-09-03
Parties: Hungerford Consulting LLC d/b/a H4D Consulting ("Consultant") and
         Aspira Women's Health, Inc. ("Client")
SEC URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/926617/000164117225026441/ex10-1.htm

--- Opening ---
"MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT Number ASP0001 (this "Agreement") is dated to be
effective as of September 2, 2025, (the "Effective Date"), by and between
Hungerford Consulting LLC d/b/a H4D Consulting (hereinafter "Consultant")...
and Aspira Women's Health, Inc (hereinafter "Client,"..."

--- Termination (Section 3) ---
"This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall, unless
terminated sooner in accordance with the terms of this Agreement or extended
by agreement between the Parties, expire one (1) year after the date upon
which all Statements of Work have either expired or been completed..."

--- Auto-Renewal ---
Not present — the agreement expires one year after completion of all
Statements of Work with no automatic renewal mechanism. (Useful real-world
test case for correctly reporting "not found" rather than guessing.)

--- Confidentiality (Section 9) ---
"The Receiving Party agrees not to use any Confidential Information of the
Disclosing Party for any purpose except in connection with business
relationship and services provided by and between the Parties."

--- Indemnification (Section 7) ---
"Consultant agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold Client, its owners,
directors, officers, employees and agents harmless from any and all third
party demands, claims, actions, suits, proceedings, costs, expenses, damages
and liabilities..."

--- Limitation of Liability ---
Not present in this agreement. (Another real "not found" case.)

--- Governing Law (Section 18) ---
"THIS AGREEMENT...SHALL IN ALL RESPECTS BE GOVERNED BY AND INTERPRETED,
CONSTRUED, AND DETERMINED IN ACCORDANCE WITH, THE APPLICABLE PROVISIONS OF
THE INTERNAL LAWS OF THE STATE OF TEXAS..."

--- Payment Terms (Section 4) ---
"Invoices will be submitted semi-monthly on the 15th and 30th of each month
in US Dollars (USD) and payment shall be remitted in USD by ACH...Invoices
shall be payable within five (5) days of receipt."

CONTRACT 2: NON-COMPETITION, NON-SOLICITATION AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
==========================================================
Source: LSI Industries Inc. — 8-K exhibit EX-10.2
Filed: 2017-02-27
Parties: LSI Industries Inc. ("LSI") and Dennis W. Wells ("Executive")
SEC URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/763532/000089225117000038/form8k022717_ex10-2.htm

Note: this is an executive non-compete/non-disclosure agreement (individual,
not a business-to-business mutual NDA) — included because it's a real,
clean, publicly-filed example, but it stresses the tool differently than a
standard commercial NDA (no payment terms, no limitation-of-liability
section, by design).

--- Opening ---
"This Non-Competition, Non-Solicitation and Non-Disclosure Agreement (this
"Agreement") is made this 24th day of February, 2017, by and among LSI
Industries Inc., an Ohio corporation ("LSI") and Dennis W. Wells
("Executive")."

--- Confidentiality Scope ---
Covers "trade secrets, proprietary data and confidential information related
to the Business" including customer lists, research programs, financial
information, pricing methods, and intellectual property arrangements.

--- Indemnification ---
"LSI agrees to indemnify and hold Executive harmless from and against his
refusal to submit to a demand to disclose such Restricted Information and
all fees, costs, or expenses incurred by him (including reasonable
attorneys fees and disbursements)."

--- Limitation of Liability ---
Not present in this document.

--- Governing Law ---
"This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with, and the rights of the
parties shall be governed by, the laws of the State of Ohio."

--- Payment Terms ---
Not present in this document (expected — not a commercial services contract).
