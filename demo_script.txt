DEMO SCRIPT — Contract Clause Extractor & Risk/Renewal Tracker
For: screen recording to send to Andrew
Total runtime: ~3.5 minutes at a natural pace (cuttable sections marked)

====================================================================
1. OPENING (10 sec)
====================================================================
"Hi Andrew — after our conversation, I wanted to show you something
rather than just talk about it. This is a Contract Clause Extractor
and Risk Tracker — upload a contract, it pulls out the key clauses,
checks them against a legal playbook, flags anything risky, drafts
fixes, and tracks renewal deadlines across your whole portfolio.
Let me walk through the whole thing."

====================================================================
2. HOW I BUILT IT (15 sec) — simple, plain terms
====================================================================
"Quick note on how I built this — I used Claude Code, Anthropic's AI
coding assistant, to actually write, test, and fix the code with me,
back and forth, the same way I'd work with a developer. I think
you'll appreciate that part especially.

Separately — for the contract analysis itself, inside the app — I
did consider using Claude's API too, but it's pricier per contract,
so for this build I went with OpenAI's models instead. That's part
of why I wanted to show you the cost comparison coming up."

====================================================================
3. UPLOAD & FIRST LOOK (15 sec)
====================================================================
"I'll upload this one — actually a real Master Services Agreement,
pulled straight from a public SEC filing, not something I wrote
myself. [upload sidebar, pick model, click Analyze Contract] It runs
through the extraction now — one API call per contract."

====================================================================
4. RISK SCORE & KEY DATES (15 sec)
====================================================================
"Right at the top: a Risk Score — 0 to 100, color-coded, a quick
triage signal before anyone reads the details. Below that, the key
dates it pulled out — effective date, renewal date, termination
notice deadline — each one with the exact quoted text it came from,
so nothing here is guessed."

====================================================================
5. CLAUSE CARDS + SUGGESTED REDLINE (20 sec)
====================================================================
"Clauses are sorted flagged-first, so the risky stuff is what you
see immediately, not buried alphabetically. Here's indemnification,
flagged as one-sided — and right below the reason, a suggested
replacement clause, drafted from the playbook's acceptable language.
So it's not just flagging problems, it's proposing the fix."

====================================================================
6. LAWYER / PLAIN-ENGLISH TOGGLE  <-- sell this one hardest (15 sec)
====================================================================
"This part ties directly to what you said the job actually is —
translating for non-technical people. [flip the toggle] Same exact
analysis, but in Plain-English mode it reads like something you'd
hand a client with zero legal background. Flip back to Legal mode
and it's precise, citation-style language for internal counsel."

====================================================================
7. MEMO EXPORT (10 sec) — cuttable if short on time
====================================================================
"One click generates an actual Word memo — risk score, flagged
issues, suggested redlines, a disclaimer — something you could send
to a client instead of screen-sharing a dashboard. [click Download,
briefly show the opened doc]"

====================================================================
8. DASHBOARD — KPIs (10 sec)
====================================================================
"Switching to the dashboard. These tiles track every contract I've
uploaded by urgency — overdue, due in 30/60/90 days — across the
whole portfolio, not just one document."

====================================================================
9. ASK YOUR PORTFOLIO (15 sec)
====================================================================
"I can ask it questions in plain English across everything I've
uploaded — [type a query like 'which contracts have no limitation of
liability clause'] — and it checks every saved contract and tells me
which ones match, with the reasoning."

====================================================================
10. UPCOMING DEADLINES + RENEWAL EMAIL (20 sec)
====================================================================
"Below that, every upcoming deadline across all contracts. And for
any of these, [pick one from the dropdown] it drafts a renewal
action email — subject and body already written — and [click Open
in Email App] this opens it directly in my own email app, ready to
review and send. I didn't want the AI actually sending emails on its
own — that stays a human decision — but it gets you from 'deadline
noticed' to 'email drafted and ready' in one click."

====================================================================
11. PORTFOLIO HEAT-MAP (10 sec) — cuttable if short on time
====================================================================
"And at the bottom, a heat-map — every contract against every clause
type, color-coded — so if I'm managing a dozen vendor contracts, I
can see at a glance which ones have problems and where."

====================================================================
12. THE COST/TRUST MOMENT  <-- NEVER CUT THIS (30 sec)
====================================================================
"This is the part I actually wanted your take on. Quick plain-terms
explainer first: there are two 'brains' this can run on. GPT-4o-mini
is the smaller, cheaper, faster version — think of it as skimming
the contract. GPT-4o is the full-size version — it reads everything
more carefully, but costs more per contract.

[switch model to gpt-4o-mini, re-analyze the same contract] I ran
this exact same contract through the mini model five separate times
— and it missed a real red flag every single time. [switch to
gpt-4o, re-analyze] The full model caught it every time, same
contract, same code — only the model changed.

So the tool lets you choose, live, and I think that's the real
decision every client-facing build like this comes down to: cost
versus trust."

====================================================================
13. CLOSE (15 sec)
====================================================================
"This can also be extended to run on Claude's API directly, or on
OpenRouter for open-source models — more options to compare cost and
trust depending on what a client actually needs.

It's live at [Hugging Face link] if you want to try it on one of
your own documents. Let me know what you think."
